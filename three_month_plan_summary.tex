This three-month plan focuses on applying the Probabilistic Error Cancellation (PEC) package to real quantum devices in the first month, followed by two months of English journal paper writing. During the first month, the main objective is to deploy the PEC workflow on real hardware, complete backend selection, circuit execution, calibration, and preliminary evaluation on benchmark circuits such as Bell-state, CNOT, and teleportation experiments.

During the second and third months, the focus shifts to writing the journal paper in English. The second month will be used to organize experimental data, generate figures and tables, and draft the introduction, methodology, and results sections. The third month will concentrate on completing the discussion and conclusion, polishing the full manuscript, improving academic language, and preparing the paper for advisor review or journal submission.
